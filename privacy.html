TaxSnap AI — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 • Made by Sufyan Ramzan, Lahore

1. What we collect

Receipts you scan, income you add, mileage trips, and account email (if you use Supabase login). We do NOT sell your data.

2. On-device OCR — Photo never leaves your phone

OCR runs on-device with Tesseract. Your receipt photo is NOT uploaded to our backend unless you turn on Supabase Sync. If Sync is ON, images are stored in your Supabase account only.

3. Currency & Tax

Currency is locked by country (UK = £, USA = $). EUR receipts are converted using the rate you set. HMRC / IRS API is NOT connected — exports are MTD-ready CSV only.

4. Your rights (GDPR)

You can export (CSV/PDF/JSON) or delete all data anytime: Settings → Delete Account. Email us to request deletion.
